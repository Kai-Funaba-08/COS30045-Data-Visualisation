Question 1

How did national counts change from 2011 to 2021, and what happened around 2020?

Road crash hospitalisations rose from 34,033 in 2011 to 39,866 in 2019, an increase of 17.1%. They fell 4.8% to 37,966 in 2020, then recovered to 39,505 in 2021.

Hospitalisations per year, Australia

Source: BITRE hospitalised injury data, cleaned in KNIME (national_annual.csv, national_monthly.csv).

The sharpest monthly drop came in April 2020, with 2,357 hospitalisations, 32.4% below April 2019. This dip coincides with the first COVID-19 restrictions, when far fewer people were travelling. The data shows timing only; it cannot show that the restrictions caused the fall.
