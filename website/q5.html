Question 5

How do First Nations patterns compare, within the data’s limits?

First Nations hospitalisations rose 90.2% from 2011 to 2021, against 14.9% for non-Indigenous Australians. Where people live is also very different: in 2021, 23.0% of First Nations people hospitalised lived in remote areas, against 1.7% of non-Indigenous Australians.
