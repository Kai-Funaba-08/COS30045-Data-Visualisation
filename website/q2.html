Question 2

How do males and females compare, and how did each change in 2020?

Males were 65.3% of people hospitalised in 2021. In 2020, female hospitalisations fell 11.8% while male hospitalisations fell only 0.7%. That pattern coincides with COVID-19 restrictions, but the data can’t show the cause.

By age group: change from 2011 to 2021

Ages 65–74 rose 53.4% and ages 17–25 fell 1.0%, against 16.1% for all ages. The age data isn’t split by sex, so this is shown separately.

Source: BITRE hospitalised injury data, cleaned in KNIME (sex_annual.csv, age_annual.csv). A small group recorded as intersex, indeterminate or missing (0–7 a year) is left out.
