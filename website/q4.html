Question 4

Which road users drove the change from 2011 to 2021?

Hospitalisations rose by 5,472, from 34,033 in 2011 to 39,505 in 2021. Pedal cyclists rose by 2,772 (+51.4%), which is about half of that rise. Car drivers (+21.7%) and motorcyclists (+17.4%) also grew, while pedestrians fell 15.3% and car passengers fell 7.4%.

Source: BITRE hospitalised injury data, cleaned in KNIME (road_user_annual.csv).
