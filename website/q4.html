Question 4

How do state and territory trends differ, and what do raw counts hide?

Queensland rose 65.2% from 2011 to 2021, with no data break noted. Victoria rose 37.4% from 2012. NSW fell from 10,618 to 9,003, but a change in definition affects that comparison. Shaded areas mark these data breaks.

Hospitalisations by state and territory, 2011–2021

Source: BITRE hospitalised injury data; population from ABS (state_annual.csv).

The eight states and territories don’t add up to the national total (33,553 against 34,033 in 2011). That’s expected, because the national figure also includes people with an unrecorded or migratory residence.
