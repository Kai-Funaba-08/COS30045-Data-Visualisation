Question 2

Which road users drove the change?

Cyclists rose by 2,772 between 2011 and 2021, which is 50.7% of the net rise of 5,472. Their share went from 15.8% to 20.7%. In 2020, cyclists rose 14.1% while car drivers fell 9.4% and pedestrians fell 22.5%.

Question 3

Who is most affected by age and sex?

From 2011 to 2021, hospitalisations of people aged 65–74 rose 53.4%, while ages 17–25 fell 1.0%, against 16.1% overall.

Question 4

How do state and territory trends differ, and what do raw counts hide?

Queensland rose 65.2% from 2011 to 2021. Victoria rose 37.4% from 2012. NSW fell from 10,618 to 9,003, but a change in definition affects that comparison. Shaded areas mark these data breaks.

Question 5

How do First Nations patterns compare, within the data’s limits?

First Nations hospitalisations rose 90.2% from 2011 to 2021, against 14.9% for non-Indigenous Australians. In 2020 they rose 23.4% while non-Indigenous counts fell 6.1%.
