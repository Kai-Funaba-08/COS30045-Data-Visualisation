Question 3

Who is most affected by age and sex?

From 2011 to 2021, hospitalisations of people aged 65–74 rose 53.4%, while ages 17–25 fell 1.0%, against 16.1% overall.

Hospitalisations by age group and year

Hover a cell for the count. The 2021 column shows the change since 2011.

Males were 65.3% of 2021 cases. In 2020, female hospitalisations fell 11.8% but male hospitalisations fell only 0.7%.
