Reading the data

  • The data shows patterns, not causes. The 2020 changes coincide with COVID-19 restrictions; they are not shown to be caused by them.
  • Most charts use counts, so differences in population size and age structure explain part of the gaps between groups.
  • Victoria and New South Wales have data breaks, which are marked † on the state map.
  • Better recording of First Nations status over time may also contribute to the rise in First Nations counts.
